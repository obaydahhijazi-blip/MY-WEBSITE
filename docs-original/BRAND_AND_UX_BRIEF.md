# Creative direction — grounded brief

**Client:** Obaydah Hijazi / عبيدة حجازي. Independent personal portfolio, not agency.

**Primary objective:** Credibility + more direct client enquiries and 30-minute consultations through WhatsApp.

**Stylistic balance:** restrained formal editorial minimalism with very measured creative gestures. The hero can be cinematic, but the rest of the site is light and disciplined.

**Palette:** petroleum #001E25, deep teal #00373E, cyan #00BBD4, yellow #FFBD06, warm offwhite #FAFAF8, charcoal #111719.

**Hero:** use the isolated cut-out portrait, an English wordmark matching the personal cover's right side, logo, and at most 2–3 decorative layers. If portrait already has dark/cyan halo, avoid piling up filters. Site's primary meaning must remain accessible Arabic HTML text.

**Sections:** 1 Hero; 2 About; 3 Expertise; 4 Clients/Collaborations; 5 Selected Work; 6 Free consultation 30 min; 7 Contact.

**Personal bio:** factual, concise, credible. Refer to artistic direction, graphic design, content creation and creative AI, collaborating on commercial, advertising and media projects in Arab markets. Do not elevate a CV-listed organization to a confirmed client without approval.

**Audience:** Arab clients, particularly companies in GCC; tone is standard Arabic with no flamboyant sales claims or excessive decoration.

**Art-direction inspirations:** https://ghonimyarts.com/ and https://thmanyah.com/ — inspiration only, do not recreate their UI.

**Interaction ambition:** pointer parallax for isolated hero only, keyboard-friendly/lightweight transitions elsewhere, reduced motion. Make mobile feel deliberately art-directed rather than cropped desktop.

**Pending content:** logos from clients, curated works & captions, confirmed public email, consultation availability/provider link, WhatsApp Business notifications credentials.
